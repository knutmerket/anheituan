This is a higher grade **Zhongcha (中茶)** heizhuan. Produced for Sanli ([湖南省三利茶叶进出口有限公司](https://baike.baidu.com/item/湖南省三利茶叶进出口有限公司/9443729)) and supposedly meant for export to Japan.

A couple of other things sets this apart from most big-factoruy productions from that era: The relatively tender material used (seems to be a mix og tian- and gongjian material, but more stems than the 2008 bricks which brings out a sweeter profile), the grade consistency throghout the brick (a more typical situation is a brick that is either crude throughout or a finer exterior wrapped around a crude core), the relatively small brick size (a big reason I decided to put these up in the first place). The profile of this one leands towards fruity notes, with some almond in the background (less prominent than the 2008 brick).

A robust tea, the highlight for me of both this and the 2008 brick is the clean aged taste and body feeling they produce.
